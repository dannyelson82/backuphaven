# Logging in

## First start

The first time you open BackupHaven it asks you to **create the admin account**:

1. **Username**: `admin` is filled in; change it if you like.
2. **Password**: at least 10 characters. A short sentence works well.
3. **Confirm password**, then **Create account**.

You're logged in straight away. There are no default passwords, and only one
admin account can be created this way. Do this before making BackupHaven
reachable from the internet.

## Logging in

Enter your username and password and click **Log in**. Password managers can
fill in and save the form.

- **Keep me logged in** (ticked by default) keeps you logged in on this device
  until you log out, or until you haven't visited for 7 days. Untick it on a
  shared computer: you're logged out when the browser closes.
- After a few wrong passwords, BackupHaven makes you wait before trying again,
  and the wait grows with each further attempt.

**Log out** (on the home page) ends the session on this device.
